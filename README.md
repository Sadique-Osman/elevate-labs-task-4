# 📊 Task 4 — Financial Sales Dashboard

## 🎯 Objective
Design an interactive 2-page financial dashboard for business stakeholders
using Power BI Desktop, based on real sales data from Microsoft Financials dataset.

---

## 📁 Dataset
- **File:** Microsoft Financials.csv
- **Source:** Kaggle (Open Dataset)
- **Rows:** 700 records
- **Time Period:** 2013 – 2014
- **Countries:** USA, Canada, Germany, France, Mexico
- **Products:** Paseo, VTT, Velo, Amarilla, Montana, Carretera
- **Segments:** Government, Small Business, Enterprise, Midmarket, Channel Partners

---

## 📌 Dashboard Pages

### Page 1 — Sales Overview
- 4 KPI Cards: Total Sales, Total Profit, Units Sold, Total COGS
- Monthly Profit Trend (Line Chart)
- Monthly Sales Trend (Line Chart)
- Sales by Segment (Bar Chart)
- Year Slicer for interactivity

### Page 2 — Product & Country Analysis
- Top 4 Products by Profit (Bar Chart)
- Sales and Profit by Product (Clustered Bar Chart)
- Sales by Country (Map Visual)
- Segment Slicer for filtering

---

## 📊 Key KPIs

| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | 117.87M |
| Total Profit | 16.89M |
| Units Sold | 1.12M |
| Total COGS | 100.98M |
| Profit Margin % | ~14.3% |

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Paseo** is the top-performing product with 4.8M in profit
- **Government** segment drives the highest sales at 53M
- **October** is the peak sales month at 21.7M
- **COGS** is 100.98M vs Sales of 117.87M — tight margin of 14.3%

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques
- **Power BI Desktop** — dashboard creation
- **DAX** — custom measure for Profit Margin %
- **Visuals used** — KPI Cards, Line Charts, Bar Charts, Map, Slicers
- **Features** — Top N filter, Page navigation, Consistent dark theme
